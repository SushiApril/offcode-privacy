# MyStack Privacy Policy

Updated November 5, 2026. Applies to MyStack version 1.0.0, developed by Sushant Gupta.

## Data collection

MyStack does not collect or transmit personal data, gameplay activity, or device identifiers to the developer. The app contains no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs and does not require an account.

## On-device storage

Your best score, appearance selections, and sound and haptic preferences are stored on your device. MyStack does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Device backups are managed by Apple according to your device settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data; restoring a device backup may restore that data.

## Purchases and access

MyStack is free and has no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Gameplay works offline. Opening a support or privacy link uses your browser and may require an internet connection.

## Support

Support is available through the same public GitHub issue tracker used for OffCode. Please identify MyStack in your request. Information you choose to submit to GitHub is handled by GitHub; avoid including personal information in public issues.

[Contact support](https://github.com/SushiApril/offcode-privacy/issues).

## Changes

Any future changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in this policy.
